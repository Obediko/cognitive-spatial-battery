# CognitiveBA3 Academic and Technical References

These references describe conceptual, methodological, statistical, stimulus, and software foundations. Citation does not authorize redistribution of protected assessment materials, establish equivalence between independently developed tasks and published instruments, or validate the CognitiveBA3 implementation.

## Cognitive assessment and reference architecture

Weintraub, S., Besser, L., Dodge, H. H., Teylan, M., Ferris, S., Goldstein, F. C., Giordani, B., Kramer, J., Loewenstein, D., Marson, D., Mungas, D., Salmon, D., Welsh-Bohmer, K., Zhou, X. H., Shirk, S. D., Atri, A., Kukull, W. A., Phelps, C., & Morris, J. C. (2018). Version 3 of the Alzheimer Disease Centers' Neuropsychological Test Battery in the Uniform Data Set (UDS). *Alzheimer Disease & Associated Disorders, 32*(1), 10-17. https://doi.org/10.1097/WAD.0000000000000223

National Alzheimer's Coordinating Center. (2015). *Uniform Data Set version 3: Forms and documentation*. https://www.naccdata.org/data-forms-and-documentation/udsv3-forms-and-documentation

Tombaugh, T. N., Kozak, J., & Rees, L. (1999). Normative data stratified by age and education for two measures of verbal fluency: FAS and animal naming. *Archives of Clinical Neuropsychology, 14*(2), 167-177. https://doi.org/10.1093/arclin/14.2.167

Gollan, T. H., Weissberger, G. H., Runnqvist, E., Montoya, R. I., & Cera, C. M. (2012). Self-ratings of spoken language dominance: A Multilingual Naming Test (MINT) and preliminary norms for young and aging Spanish-English bilinguals. *Bilingualism: Language and Cognition, 15*(3), 594-615. https://doi.org/10.1017/S1366728911000332

The NACC and MINT publications are cited for scientific context. CognitiveBA3 does not contain or claim to reproduce their protected forms, official item sets, score norms, or validated administration procedures.

## Spatial cognition and navigation

Doeller, C. F., Barry, C., & Burgess, N. (2010). Evidence for grid cells in a human memory network. *Nature, 463*, 657-661. https://doi.org/10.1038/nature08704

Coughlan, G., Laczó, J., Hort, J., Minihane, A. M., & Hornberger, M. (2018). Spatial navigation deficits: Overlooked cognitive marker for preclinical Alzheimer disease? *Nature Reviews Neurology, 14*, 496-506. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41582-018-0031-x

Laczó, M., Martinkovic, L., Lerch, O., Wiener, J. M., Kalinova, J., Matuskova, V., Nedelska, Z., Vyhnalek, M., Hort, J., & Laczó, J. (2022). Different profiles of spatial navigation deficits in Alzheimer's disease biomarker-positive versus biomarker-negative older adults with amnestic mild cognitive impairment. *Frontiers in Aging Neuroscience, 14*, 886778. https://doi.org/10.3389/fnagi.2022.886778

These studies justify interest in spatial constructs. They do not show that CognitiveBA3 object-location or pointing scores identify a particular brain structure, predict disease, or reproduce the cited paradigms.

## Stimulus development and visual naming

Brodeur, M. B., Dionne-Dostie, E., Montreuil, T., & Lepage, M. (2010). The Bank of Standardized Stimuli (BOSS), a new set of 480 normative photos of objects to be used as visual stimuli in cognitive research. *PLOS ONE, 5*(5), e10773. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0010773

The open-access status of the BOSS paper does not, by itself, establish the reuse terms of every image in the external stimulus bank. Item-level provenance and primary licence evidence remain necessary.

## Reliability and cross-language adaptation

Koo, T. K., & Li, M. Y. (2016). A guideline of selecting and reporting intraclass correlation coefficients for reliability research. *Journal of Chiropractic Medicine, 15*(2), 155-163. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcm.2016.02.012

Bland, J. M., & Altman, D. G. (1986). Statistical methods for assessing agreement between two methods of clinical measurement. *The Lancet, 327*(8476), 307-310. https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(86)90837-8

Beaton, D. E., Bombardier, C., Guillemin, F., & Ferraz, M. B. (2000). Guidelines for the process of cross-cultural adaptation of self-report measures. *Spine, 25*(24), 3186-3191. https://doi.org/10.1097/00007632-200012150-00014

Beaton et al. address self-report measures rather than this exact cognitive battery. Their translation principles are relevant to structured language review, but task-specific equivalence still requires an independently justified validation design.
